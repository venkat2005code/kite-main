Our Story

Built by Pilots, Tuned by the Wind

Since 2014 we have designed, stitched and flight-tested every kite from a converted boat shed on the Bodega Bay waterfront. Our workshop sits where Pacific thermals meet the ridge, so every wing is proven in real coastal air before it ships.

2014 Founded
48,000+ Kites Flown
32 Crew & Pilots
Why We Fly

Master the Wind, Defy the Gravity

We believe a good kite should feel like an extension of your hands. That means honest wind ratings, spars you can replace in the field, and ripstop that still holds its colour after five summers of salt and sun.

Everything we sell, from a child’s first diamond to a 6-metre traction foil, is flown by our own crew before it earns a place in the catalog.

air

Real-Air Tested

Every design logs a minimum of 40 flight hours across Beaufort 2 to 6 before release. No wind tunnel shortcuts.

build

Repairable by Design

Standard spar sizes, stocked bridle kits and a lifetime of spare parts. A broken kite should be a Saturday fix, not landfill.

eco

Coast-Friendly Materials

Solvent-free coatings, recycled ripstop in our family range and sand stakes cast from reclaimed aluminium.

groups

Community First

We sponsor 14 coastal fly-ins a year and run free Saturday pilot clinics at the shop. Bring a kite or borrow one of ours.

Milestones

A Decade on the Ridge

2014

The Boat Shed

Two pilots, one sewing machine and a rented boat shed at Bodega Bay. First run: 120 hand-cut delta kites.

2017

Zephyr Pro Launches

Our first carbon-framed dual-line stunt kite. Still the most requested wing in the lineup today.

2021

Traction & Beach Gear

Power foils, aerodynamic shelters and anemometers join the range as the crew grows to 20.

2025

Certified Manufacturer

Certified Wind Gear Manufacturer status and a second test field opened on the Atlantic coast at Cape May.
