Respecting the Coastal Fringe: Dune Grass & Shorebird Stewardship

Coastal dunes form our coastlines' front line of defense against ocean storms. As flight pilots and beach enthusiasts, we share an obligation to preserve nesting habitats and avoid eroding fragile root systems.

Protect Marram Grass Roots
Always pitch windbreaks and anchors on open intertidal or foredune sand. Never drive stakes directly through dune vegetation.
